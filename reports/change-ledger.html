Three findings that contradict the paper

These are not speedups. They bear on whether the reported results mean what they are said to mean.

1. The headline comparison gives HadNet twice the storage

HadNet at rank r stores four factors — 2r(m+n) values. SVD at rank r stores r(m+n). The paper compares at matched rank, so HadNet gets double the budget. Give SVD the same budget by letting it use rank 2r:

Paper's comparison, rank 25
HadNet 0.12036
SVD(25) 0.14382  HadNet wins
Same storage, rank 25
HadNet 0.12036
SVD(50) 0.06435  SVD wins

SVD wins at every rank on both images tested, and the gap widens — at rank 128 it is 0.00003 against 0.00507. On a natural image HadNet barely wins even at matched rank, so twice the parameters buys nothing. Caveats: two images, and a 2000-epoch budget that may under-train.

2. HadDNN's networks are actively harmful

Each factor network maps a factor to itself through a 64-unit bottleneck — but the final layer's bias is already full-dimensional, so it alone spans the whole factor space. The 332M weights upstream add a 64-dim correction to something already complete.

Monotone in the wrong direction: freezing 98.5% of the parameters halves the error; widening the bottleneck doubles it again. Plain factors win at every learning rate from 0.3 to 0.001. At rank 25 on video the networks need 4 GB before activations — it OOMs on a 6 GB card, which is why HadDNN has no rank-25 row in my sweep. The paper's own Table 9 already shows it dominated; this is the mechanism.

3. The benchmark rewards fitting the injected noise

The scripts add 0.01·randn and then score error against the noisy tensor. At rank 25 the full run gives:

Error vs noisy target (reported)
0.01621
Error vs the true video
0.02361

The reconstruction is closer to the noise realisation than to the real signal. At rank 10 the ordering reverses, because the model then lacks capacity to memorise noise. So every comparison at rank 20+ partly measures noise-fitting, and a method that overfits harder scores better. The same three lines also push 17.79% of an MRI below zero, after which metrics are computed on clamped output.

Hardware differsRTX 4050 (6 GB mobile) vs the paper's RTX 4500 Ada (24 GB). Ratios transfer; absolute times do not.
Baselines not like-for-likeTheir AGD/IGD run 40,000 iterations on numba-CPU against HadNet's 2,000 epochs on GPU. The reported 21× advantage over TAGD is close to the iteration ratio alone — this is in the released code, not something I introduced.
Two images for the rank findingVideo and the real-world matrices are untested, and the effect is already visibly data-dependent.
